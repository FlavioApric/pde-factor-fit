\section{The extended specification, step by step}\label{app:spec}
This appendix renders the specification of the extended pipeline (originally written in Italian, kept in \texttt{claude/notes/ALGORITMO\_ESTESO\_it.md}) as one table per step. The specification introduces no new claim: it composes claims of the proof packages, each quoted with its status (proved; proved modulo a cited theorem; from a verified source; conditional on a verification; open; false).
A step is \emph{grounded} only on proved claims or on claims from a source whose theorem number was verified; a step that uses an unverified source is marked ``conditional on verification''. Identifiers such as C3-T1, P4-T2, VAR-T1 refer to the claim ledger of the analytical branch.

\paragraph{The pipeline in two layers}
\begin{verbatim}
CONDITIONING (discrete outputs, outside the convergence contract, completeness class C)
  step 1: data model -> step 2: level n (budget; UNDECIDED) -> step 3: class K and sense ID-*
CONDITIONAL CORRECTNESS "at level n, in class K" (the contract applies)
  step 4: misfit (A with enclosure, else B) / fit (B) / stop (Lim)
     -> step 5: encoder (bounded, q given: A/B with C_W^2; else C)
     -> step 6: factors, terms, interaction term (A/B/C)
  step 7: double label (completeness, correctness) + bound  sum_i (prod_{j>i} L_j) eps_i
\end{verbatim}

\subsection*{Step 1: data model (conditioning)}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.2cm}p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
input & the datum $u$ and its model declaration; for DM-$\delta$ the norm $Y$ and $\delta$; the budget (maximum level, working precision)\\
output & the fixed model; the declared coefficient ring ($\C[\partial]$, $\C(x)[\partial]$, a Weyl algebra, or a parametric class); the domain; the regularity class; the set of reachable senses\\
claims & no finite jet at one point certifies $\mathrm{Ann}(u)=(P)$ (proved); black-box identification is impossible without extra data (proved); in DM2 no correct certificate exists (proved); for exponential-polynomial data the jet of order $2d-1$ determines $\mathrm{Ann}(u)$ (proved); Bézout threshold for exponential-polynomial data (proved); $u$ exponential-polynomial implies $\sigma_p\ne\emptyset$ hence no ID-ann (proved); decidability modulo (AC) for $M2^+$ and for holonomic closed forms (proved modulo (AC)); data up to phase gives $V+c$ (proved); gauge-invariant data gives at most the gauge class (proved, source verified); the modulus of continuity of the residual in DM-$\delta$ (proved as an estimate, not a certificate)\\
failures & DM1: never ID-ann ($e^{-t}\cos x$ solves the heat equation yet $\partial_t+1$ and $\partial_x^2+1$ lie in its annihilator); DM2: no certificate (jets of order 2, 4, 6 of a non-exponential-polynomial solution coincide with those of heat polynomials); DM-$\varphi$: $V$ only modulo functions of $t$; DM-inv: $A$ only modulo gauge; DM-$\delta$ in $C^1$: no control of $V$\\
contract & conditioning: discrete output, outside the Lipschitz chain; completeness (A) for the declaration of the model (it is an input)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{Step 2: choice of the level (conditioning)}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.2cm}p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
input & the model, the budget $n_{\max}$, optional prior knowledge (known $q$, symmetries)\\
output & a level $n$ and a finite set of atoms $\mathcal D_n$; the sequence of levels to try in order of height; \UND{} at $n=n_{\max}$ without outcome\\
claims & the space of smooth functions is not separable, so no exhaustive countable dictionary exists (proved); a delay is at distance $\ge1$ from the local dictionary and equals augmented transport (proved); non-monotonicity of identification (proved); functions with a law in the dictionary form a meagre and shy set (proved); no learner identifies the annihilator on the union of principal ideals and the zero ideal (proved)\\
cost & for the linear family in $1{+}1$ dimensions, $(n+1)(n+2)/2$ monomials of degree $\le n$; for other families no general estimate is proved\\
failures & the choice of level is a heuristic (completeness class C); a nonlocal atom is never reached by the local dictionary; a generic $u$ has no law\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{Step 3: identification (conditioning of the class)}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.2cm}p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
3a scalar, constant & input $u$ in DM1, M3-hol, $M2^+$ or DM3, level $n$; output $\mathrm{Ann}(u)\cap R_{\le n}$, the gcd $G_u$, the point spectrum, the decision ID-ann (not in DM1) or relative ID-ker (DM1). Claims: $\mathrm{Ann}(u)=(P)$ iff $u,\partial_tu,\dots,\partial_t^{k-1}u$ are $\C[\partial_x]$-independent for $P$ monic in $\tau$ (proved); the test $w_i\notin\mathrm{EP}$; $G_u$ from any annihilator with an EP test; $\mathrm{Ann}(u)=(P)$ iff $\sigma_p=\emptyset$ and $G_u\sim P$; $u_t=a(\partial_x)u$ identifies relatively in the class $\deg a\le n$; elimination (proved); the algorithm and its polynomial cost are a written proof not yet audited ((A) conditional on it). Failure: $\mathrm{Ann}(\cdot)$ is discontinuous everywhere (proved).\\
3b systems & input $u\in\mathcal A^r$ and a class of systems; output an existence test of an identifying $u$, and for a given $u$ the decision ID-ann or ID-ker in the Hamiltonian class, plus the component criterion. Claims: existence of an identifying solution on arbitrary $\Omega$ (proved by an own proof, not depending on Oberst for the truth value; $\R^n$ known from Oberst 1993, read only in the abstract; convex $\Omega$ from Oberst 1990, verified); socle obstruction (known); $\det P\ne0$, $n\ge2$ implies an identifying $u$ (proved); Lam\'e with $\rho,\mu,\lambda+\mu,\lambda+2\mu\ne0$ (proved; false for $\lambda=-2\mu$); Dirac in the fixed basis $\alpha=\sigma_x$, $\beta=\sigma_z$ (proved); magnetic case an affine family. Two senses of the Dirac hypothesis: ID-ann (second component not exponential-polynomial) and ID-ker (rank 8 in the Hamiltonian class; fails with one plane wave, holds with $\ge4$ generic modes). Cost: $2r^2$ unknowns (8 for Dirac). Failures: socle $\ge2$; the sum of kernels is not enough without hypotheses on $N$; too few modes for ID-ker; magnetic: only ID-fam. Novelty is conditional on the citation audit.\\
3c variable & input $u$ in DM3, M3-hol or DM-$\varphi$, declared coefficient ring, regularity class; output $V$ through the residual map $\rho_u$, or the affine set of compatible coefficients. Claims: vacuity in a differentially closed field (proved), so the coefficient field must be fixed; for $V(x)$ every $u\not\equiv0$ in the strong class identifies $V$; for $V(t,x)$ outside the silence bands iff $\mathrm{int}\,Z(u)=\emptyset$ (continuous) or $\lvert Z(u)\rvert=0$ ($L^\infty$); Airy; unique continuation (source verified); the ID-ker version under the hypothesis that a law of the class exists. Conditional on verification: the socle/Loewy structure of the Galois route. Failures: the Tychonoff series (false); silence bands; $x\partial_t$ is never identified.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{Step 4: misfit certificate or best fit (correctness)}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.2cm}p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
input & $u$ in DM3 (or DM3$^\delta(Y)$); level and class; guide atom $D_0$; the span $W_n=\mathrm{span}(D_1u,\dots,D_Nu)$ in $Y=L^2$ or $H^s$; working precision $p$\\
output & \MIS{}(certificate) if $r_n>0$ is certified, else \FC{}$(n,r_n,\text{coefficients with bound})$\\
claims & the distance as a Hahn--Banach dual; $r_n^2=\det G(D_0u,D_1u,\dots)/\det G(D_1u,\dots)$; a weak form robust when $\sigma_{N+1}(A(\tilde u))>\lVert\delta\rVert_F$ (semi-decision); a misfit is open with a finite certificate, a fit is closed with empty interior; a fit is not certifiable from finite exact data (differential atoms); $\lvert c-\tilde c\rvert\le2\eta/\sqrt{\lambda_{\min}}$; the stopping rule R1 stops at $n_0$ under (H-ID); stability with Lipschitz constant $2\delta/\rho$ at a fixed level; the discrepancy rule selects $m=d$ with a threshold $\delta_0$ that cannot be bounded below from the data. Conditional on verification: one branch of the limit statement, one cited bound\\
computation & $r_n$ as a least-squares residual (QR, or normal equations followed by the direct residual $\lVert D_0u+\sum c_iD_iu\rVert$), \emph{not} as a ratio of Gram determinants, which is singular when $D_0u$ lies in the span\\
cost & Gram $(N+1)\times(N+1)$: $O(N^2)$ inner products each a quadrature on $\Omega$; QR $O(N^3)$; working precision $p\gtrsim\log_{10}(1/\lambda_{\min})$ digits (25 in the example)\\
contract & misfit (A) only with a rigorous enclosure of $r_n$, else (B); fit (B) with the bound at the fixed level; stop (Lim)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{Step 5: analytic encoder for $V$ (correctness)}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.2cm}p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
role & the encoder is a \emph{translation} at known $q$, not a source of information: it carries a variable-coefficient problem to a constant-coefficient one, where steps 3a and 4 apply, and carries certificates back; in the non-autonomous form it satisfies $W(t)(i\partial_t-H(t))=(i\partial_t-H_0-c(t))W(t)$\\
bounded (Volterra) & symmetric Volterra $W_q$: $\lVert W\rVert,\lVert W^{-1}\rVert\le C_W$; (A) for the exact intertwining, (B) for transported certificates with factor $C_W^2$, only if $q$ is exact; $C_W=1+a^2Qe^{a\sqrt Q}$ with $Q=\lVert q\rVert_\infty$; the kernel $K$ from a Goursat problem on the characteristic triangle, $O(M^2)$ evaluations of $q$ (indicative)\\
Darboux & differential $A$, unbounded, with nontrivial kernel: class (C); bound modes are lost and an identifying datum can become exponential-polynomial\\
claims & existence, uniqueness and stability of the Volterra transformation; $P(\partial_t,L_q)u=0$ iff $P(\partial_t,L_0)(Wu)=0$; differential $W$ are Darboux chains (prior art verified); no $t$-independent encoder for a generic $V(t,x)$ (proved); the blind-encoder statement is false and reduces to joint identification of $(q,u)$\\
bound loss & $C_W$ is the only proved bound and grows as $e^{a\sqrt Q}$: at $a\sqrt Q=5$, $C_W^2\approx1.4\times10^7$ while $\cosh^2(a\sqrt Q)\approx5.5\times10^3$, so transported certificates may lose four orders of magnitude (a tighter bound is open)\\
failures & unknown $q$: no information; $q$ estimated from step 3c: the Lipschitz constant of $q\mapsto W_q$ is not explicit, so transport is (C)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{Step 6: decomposition into factors and terms; interaction term (correctness)}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.2cm}p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
input & the law $P$ (or a candidate) with the factorisation $P=\prod Q_j$; the datum $u$\\
output & components $u_j\in\ker Q_j$; the ambiguity $\delta(P)$; interaction terms $E^{\mathrm{add}}$, $E^{\mathrm{mult}}$ with estimates\\
claims & $\ker P=\sum\ker Q_j$ on simply connected $\Omega$ with $\delta(P)=\sum_{i<j}\dim R/(Q_i,Q_j)$, unique iff the varieties are disjoint (proved); components in $H^m$ iff the sum of kernels is closed and dense, $\gN>0$ (proved); the commuting-term identity and its cost $\Theta(M2^N(L+1)^2)$ (proved); every exact selection has Lipschitz constant $\ge\gN^{-1/2}$ (proved); Burgers: jet, closed form before the shock, blow-up (proved), jumps after it (proved modulo Lax--Oleinik = Kru\v zkov, to be verified)\\
failures & \textbf{false}: the split does not preserve $H^m$ (exact loss of one derivative); on non-simply-connected $\Omega$ the split fails on the kernel of $\iota$ (defect $\le\delta(P)b_1(\Omega)$): \UND{} for the components; in $1{+}d$, $d\ge2$ the ambiguity is infinite unless the factors are comaximal; $E^{\mathrm{add}}$ is unbounded $H^m\to L^2$, so $E_{\mathrm{int}}$ from an approximate datum is (C); boundary conditions are invisible to $\mathrm{Ann}(u)$; for systems the component = factor statement needs $\mathrm{Ext}^1(N,\mathcal A)=0$\\
contract & (A) in $\mathfrak B$ and the commutative case; (B) with $\gN^{-1/2}$ or with the Faddeev remainder; (C) if $\gN$ is not estimated, for $E_{\mathrm{int}}$ from approximate data, and in the infinite-ambiguity cases\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{Step 7: convergence guarantees}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.2cm}p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
output & for each output the completeness label and the conditional-correctness label, with the bound of the chain $d(\tilde x_k,x_k)\le\sum_i(\prod_{j>i}L_j)\varepsilon_i$ computed in $O(k)$ operations by accumulating the product from the right\\
(A) & elimination (conditional on the unaudited cost proof); exactness from the gcd and the spectral criterion; commuting splitting; the Bezout criterion on the torus\\
(B) & Dyson--Phillips (one part conditional on verification); Magnus only for bounded generators in finite dimension or truncations with $\lVert H_N\rVert\tau<\pi$ (a source theorem), in infinite dimension under normality of the interaction-picture generator; Trotter--Kato splitting only for bounded or relatively bounded $V$; the proved splitting orders (Lie order 1, Strang $\Theta(h^{5/4})$ with Dirichlet conditions); the dictionary bound; stability at a fixed level\\
(C) & completeness (the choice of level); Magnus outside its hypotheses, in particular the Stark case where $\int\lVert A_I\rVert=\infty$; splitting for unbounded $V$; $\gN$ not estimated; DM-$\delta$ below an unknown threshold; encoders with estimated $q$ or of Darboux type; $E_{\mathrm{int}}$ from approximate data\\
(Lim) & identification in the limit without a bound; the stopping rule; the limit statement of the dictionary\\
failures & no limit procedure decides whether a law exists; discontinuity everywhere (hence steps 1--3 are outside the chain); the noise threshold is not certifiable; two criteria of the nonlocal and Mori--Zwanzig routes are false\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection*{What the pipeline promises (all conditional on the declared level $n$ and class $K$)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{M3-hol, DM3 with symbolic proof, $M2^+$ modulo (AC):} \texttt{IDENTIFIED[ID-ann | ID-res | ID-ker with (H-ex) | ID-fam]} in the declared class, correctness (A); in the decidable cases completeness is also (A) modulo (AC).
\item \textbf{DM1 (exponential-polynomial):} only \texttt{IDENTIFIED[ID-ker, DM1]}, relative to a finite class, correctness (A); never ID-ann.
\item \textbf{DM3 with numerical computation:} misfit (A) with a rigorous enclosure, else (B); fit compatible with a bound (B) at the fixed level; never ``law identified''.
\item \textbf{DM2:} \texttt{ID-LIM}$(n)$, contract (Lim), no certificate.
\item \textbf{DM-$\delta(Y)$:} moduli of continuity and a threshold that cannot be certified; robustness stays open.
\item \textbf{The chain 4--6 bound} holds only if every step is (A) or (B).
\end{enumerate}
\emph{Novelty (stated carefully):} not the individual steps, most of which are known, but the composition of levels L6--L10 under one two-layer contract together with the new negative results (the dictionary theorem and the fit certificate, the principal-ideal limit theorem, the Tychonoff counterexample, the encoder obstruction). The delta of the existence theorem on a non-injective $\Omega$ is not claimed as new until the source is read in full.

\subsection*{Open questions of the specification}
(1) Robustness under noise in DM-$\delta(Y)$. (2) An infinite-mode nonlocal example identifying a nonlocal atom; the fit certificate for nonlocal atoms. (3) The Sobolev-scale form of the limit statement (candidate). (4) The limit gcd without a majorant. (5) The variable-coefficient question on the zero set. (6) The general Weyl-algebra case of the annihilator criterion. (7) Unbounded $V$ in the convergence theorems. (8) Cost per level of the dictionary search; an audit of the elimination algorithm; a rigorous enclosure for the misfit. (9) The Sobolev $H^{2k}$ case and the weak form for the encoder; a bound tighter than $C_W$. (10) An explicit Lipschitz constant of $q\mapsto W_q$. (11) The principal-class form of the observation statement; non-monic symbols; Maxwell. (12) A ledger row for Trotter--Kato and closing the citations listed in the claims table.
