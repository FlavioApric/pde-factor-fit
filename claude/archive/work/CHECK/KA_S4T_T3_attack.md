**Rejection memo: S4T-T3 and the declared "Esito"**

The "teorema negativo" label is attached to the norms where the result says nothing, while the one norm where the question means something is left open. In that norm, the project's own evidence points the other way.

Items (i) and (ii) are not statements about interaction. The proof of (i) writes E^add = [u − h + u₀] − v, a continuous function minus S_B(t)u₀. E^mult_BA is likewise u minus S_B applied to smooth data. The jump D is the Lax–Oleinik jump of S_B alone, from S4T-L2(d). Any error that subtracts the entropic hyperbolic flow inherits that jump, whatever the dynamics. The Esito admits this in (a): "A9 stessa la impone". It still fires the negative branch on these items and marks "PR3 ✔ in L∞". Scoring a prediction on a tautology of the definition is not evidence.

Item (iii) is, by the authors' own word, the classical L¹ error of heat applied to a jump ("nessuna novità"). Its Θ(√ν) rate depends on [F3], which is unverified. The identification of Lax–Oleinik with Kružkov, on which every item rests, depends on [F1]: "nessuna riga verificata".

That leaves ‖E^add‖_{L¹}, the natural norm for conservation laws and the only quantity that isolates viscous–hyperbolic interaction. (vi) reduces it to the vanishing-viscosity L¹ rate, which is not proved here, and S4T-Q1 records it as open. The numerics give ‖E^add‖_{L¹}/ν ≈ 9.8–10, stable as ν decreases. That suggests order 1 in ν survives the shock, which is a positive answer.

Item (vii) does not repair this. It compares against the jet law of S4T-T1, which is valid only before t*. Extending it past the shock sets up a straw man.

**Verdict: reject.** The "negative" verdict is an artifact of definition A9 and does not hold as a finding about splitting.
