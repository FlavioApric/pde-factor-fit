# Kill-argument P4-T5 — Thread 1 (memo d'attacco), subagente Claude fresco a474f066361fd42a5, 25/10/2026, verbatim
**Rejection memo: P4-T5 (and the "Lettura" that frames P4-T4)**

I checked Passi 1–5 and found no false step. Passo 2 gives closure under derivation and V_K^⊥ = (P) ∩ R_{≤K}. Witness points in K² are available because only finite sets are excluded. Passi 3–4 match the zero-test answers by a generic choice, and in Passo 5 the adversary is chosen after the algorithm's run on u. The defect is what the result is used to establish.

The paper uses P4-T5 to support the claim that C3-Q1 reduces "esattamente" to a bound B ≥ dim(W ∩ EP), and that "il maggiorante non si può eliminare". P4-T5 proves neither.

What it shows is a standard finite-information adversary argument. "Ann(u) = (P)" is not an open condition in the topology generated by finitely many jets and finitely many zero-tests, because ker P ∩ EP is dense there (Passi 2–4). This is the generic fact that a property which is co-semi-decidable but not semi-decidable cannot be decided from finite observations. It applies to any extra datum and singles out none.

No converse is proved. Nothing shows that a decider for "Ann = (P)", with or without extra data, yields B or any upper bound on dim R/J_u. So "esattamente" is unsupported, and B could be replaced by strictly weaker information, such as a degree bound for one witness h·P/P_i. On that point, P4-T5 is consistent with both answers.

The quantifier "for every P" also rests on an unproved existence claim. Outside the single heat-equation instance (C8), the file never shows that a real-analytic u with jets in Q̄ and Ann(u) = (P) exists. Where none exists, the impossibility statement is vacuous.

Verdict: the result is correct but trivial, and it does not establish the structural claim it is cited for. Reject as stated.
